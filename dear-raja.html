Dear Raja,

I miss you more than I expected to.

It's strange how much a place changes when one person is missing from it. The days have gone on as usual, but something about them feels a little emptier without you here to share them with.

I hope your time back home has been good to you — rest well, eat well, and let the people there spoil you a little. Just know that you're thought of often, and that home will feel complete again the moment you're back in it.

With warmth and a little impatience, until you're back
